Clinical Instructions: Before & After Vaccination

Essential guidance to ensure maximum immune response and minimize adverse events.

📋

Before Your Appointment

  • ✅ Do Not Fast: Eat a light, nourishing meal before visiting the clinic to prevent dizziness or hypoglycemia.
  • ✅ Hydrate Well: Drink plenty of water before and on the day of inoculation.
  • ✅ Avoid Pre-medication: Do not preemptively take paracetamol or painkillers unless instructed by your doctor.
  • ✅ Wear Comfortable Clothing: Opt for loose, short-sleeved tops for easy upper arm deltoid access.
  • ✅ Disclose Medical History: Inform staff of severe allergies, pregnancy, blood-thinners, or recent fever episodes.
  • ✅ Bring Valid Photo ID: Carry your booking slip/SMS confirmation and registered national photo ID.
🩹

After-Booking & Post-Dose Care

  • ⏱️ Mandatory 30-Min Observation: Remain seated in the centre's waiting area for 30 minutes to detect any acute reactions.
  • 🧊 Arm Soreness: Apply a clean, cool, damp cloth to the injection site to ease local pain and swelling.
  • 💊 Managing Mild Fever: Mild low-grade fever, fatigue, or headache is normal. Paracetamol may be taken as prescribed.
  • 🚫 No Strenuous Work: Avoid vigorous physical exercise, heavy lifting, or alcohol consumption for 24 hours.
  • 📲 Automatic Next Dose: Your next scheduled dose is automatically calculated by the system and available in your portal.
  • 📥 Certificate: Download your digitally signed vaccination card from your profile dashboard.

⚠️ Post-Vaccine AEFI Emergency & Adverse Effect Support

If you or your dependent experience acute allergic symptoms, difficulty breathing, or persistent high fever (>102°F), contact our triage team immediately.

📞 Call 1075 (24/7)

🟢 Expected Mild Symptoms

Resolves autonomously within 48–72 hours:

  • Local pain, redness, or warmth at injection arm
  • Low-grade temperature (< 101°F)
  • Mild muscle ache, chills, or headache
  • Temporary tiredness or mild nausea

🔴 Warning Signs (Emergency)

Requires instant clinical intervention:

  • Shortness of breath, wheezing, or throat tightness
  • Facial swelling, lip edema, or extensive hives
  • Persistent high fever (>102°F) lasting over 3 days
  • Sudden dizziness, severe vertigo, or fainting
